# DWELLUNE — Proposed Return Rules

Prepared 14 October 2026. Customer request rules published for owner-led acceptance after approval of JazzCash and bank transfer refund methods.

- Request a return within seven days after delivery, from the original order in your account.
- Accepted reasons: damaged item, incorrect item, or change of mind.
- Change-of-mind items must be unused and include original packaging and proof of purchase. A used candle is not eligible for a change-of-mind return.
- Report damaged or incorrect items promptly to dwellunestore@gmail.com, with the order reference and clear photographs. Inspection determines eligibility; opening packaging to inspect a damaged item does not itself disqualify the complaint.
- Request approval and return instructions before sending an item. DWELLUNE covers reasonable return delivery for confirmed damaged/incorrect items; change-of-mind delivery is paid by the customer.
- Refunds require receipt and inspection of the approved returned item. For cash-on-delivery orders, support must confirm the refund amount, payout method and timing with the customer before approving the return. The account request does not send money or automatically approve a refund.
- No automatic replacement, courier booking, refund payment or gift-card credit is promised by the Phase 7 interface. These require the later fulfillment/after-sales workflows.

## Outstanding operating input

Approved refunds use JazzCash or bank transfer after inspection. Customers contact dwellunestore@gmail.com to receive a confirmed return destination before shipping. No fixed warehouse address or automatic pickup has been supplied or promised. Order placement remains a later phase.

## Research basis

Habitt requires original packaging and evidence for damaged/incorrect items and limits change-of-mind returns. Interwood describes unused/original-packaging eligibility and a 30-day return window. DWELLUNE's proposed seven-day window and cost allocation are its own proposed terms; they are not copied or represented as either retailer's exact policy.
